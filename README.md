# Domainzax

**Domain intelligence for the modern web.**

Domainzax is an experimental open-source lab for discovering, researching, evaluating, and presenting domain names as digital assets.

## What lives here

- **Domain intelligence** — structured research on names, markets, technologies, and potential use cases.
- **Name research** — repeatable frameworks for evaluating brandability, relevance, memorability, and commercial fit.
- **Buyer signals** — documented methods for identifying organizations whose products or strategies may align with a domain.
- **Experiments** — small tools and prototypes for domain research workflows.
- **Public knowledge** — reusable research notes and datasets where appropriate.

## Philosophy

A domain is more than a string. Its value depends on language, market timing, category relevance, and the clarity of the product or brand it can represent.

Domainzax focuses on evidence-driven research rather than unsupported price claims.

## Project status

Early-stage public laboratory. Expect experiments, prototypes, and evolving research methods.
