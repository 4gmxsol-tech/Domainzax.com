# Domainzax Research

This section documents the research frameworks used by Domainzax.

## Research areas

### 1. Domain quality
- Linguistic clarity
- Memorability
- Spelling and pronunciation
- Category relevance
- Extension fit

### 2. Market context
- Emerging technology categories
- Company formation and funding signals
- Product launches
- Naming patterns
- Competitive terminology

### 3. Buyer discovery
- Company-category matching
- Product/domain semantic fit
- Public signals
- Contact-path documentation
- Evidence chains

### 4. Valuation research
Domainzax separates observed market evidence from subjective estimates. Comparable sales, market demand, usage patterns, and strategic fit should be documented independently.

## Reproducibility

Research entries should record:
- date
- source
- observation
- interpretation
- confidence
- limitations
